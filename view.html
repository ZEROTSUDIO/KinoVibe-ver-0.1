Written review

Relentless practical stunt work carries this one. The story is thin but that's not really the point — every action sequence is coherent, weighty, and clearly shot. Furiosa is the real lead and the film is better for it. Docked slightly for a couple of flat dialogue scenes in the middle stretch, but the final chase more than makes up for it.
