Why Star Ratings Fail

The 4-Pillar Scoring Formula

A generic 7/10 or 3.5 stars tells you nothing. A film can feature jaw-dropping cinematography with weak dialogue, or an absurd storyline that delivers unmatched entertainment. KinoVibe captures every dimension.

📖

Story & Script

Narrative depth, screenplay, dialogue, pacing, emotional impact, and character arcs.

Score: 0.0 – 10.0
🎨

Visuals & Craft

Cinematography, color grading, art direction, framing, VFX, lighting, and camera movement.

Score: 0.0 – 10.0
⚡

Action & Intensity

Stunt choreography, suspense, set pieces, visceral energy, and physical storytelling.

Score: 0.0 – 10.0
🍿

Fun & Entertainment

Rewatchability, vibe, pure joy, emotional resonance, and how much you enjoyed the ride.

Score: 0.0 – 10.0

The Secret Sauce: Personal Bias Modifiers

No movie review is truly objective. You might love a film because of childhood nostalgia, or hate it because of an annoying plot hole. Instead of hiding your biases, KinoVibe lets you declare them transparently.

// Calculated Base Score
Base Score = (Story + Visuals + Action + Fun) / 4
// Final Score with Custom Biases
Final Score = clamp(0, 10, (Total Metrics + Total Biases) / 4)
Real World Bias Examples:
📼 Childhood 90s Nostalgia +1.0
🎵 Iconic Musical Score +0.8
🕳️ Glaring Third-Act Plot Hole -0.7
🤖 Jarring CGI Artifacts -0.5
